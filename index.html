Ask Us Directly
How early should I start the application process?

Ideally 12–18 months before your intended intake, especially for scholarship and visa-heavy destinations like the UK and Canada.

Do you help with scholarship applications?

Yes — scholarship matching and essay support is included in every counselling package, not sold as an add-on.

What is your visa success rate?

98% across the last three intakes, driven largely by our mock-interview and documentation review process.

Is the initial consultation really free?

Yes, completely — no obligation, no hidden catch. It's how we get to know your goals before recommending a path.

Can you help with accommodation once I'm accepted?

Absolutely — our accommodation desk verifies listings near campus and can book before your visa is even approved.
